# Privacy Policy — gmvmax-agency-guardrails

**Effective date:** 2026-09-09
**Publisher:** Global Trade ("we", "us")
**Applies to:** the `gmvmax-agency-guardrails` Skill package distributed via TikTok for Business Agentic Hub, and all files distributed with it.

---

## 1. Summary

This Skill is a **static instruction set** (Markdown documents and reference files). It runs
entirely inside **your** AI agent environment. **We do not receive, collect, store, process,
or transmit any data about you or your use of the Skill. No telemetry, no analytics, no
tracking, no accounts.**

## 2. What data the Skill handles — and where it stays

When your agent executes this Skill's instructions, data (such as campaign metrics, budgets,
or account identifiers) moves **between your agent and TikTok's APIs / the TikTok for
Business MCP server**, under TikTok's terms of service and your agent provider's terms.
That data never passes through, and is never visible to, the publisher of this Skill.

The Skill **never asks for, stores, transmits, or logs TikTok access tokens**. Authentication
is handled entirely by your agent platform or MCP host.

## 3. Third-party services

Use of this Skill involves, at your election and under their own respective policies:

- **TikTok for Business APIs / MCP server** — governed by TikTok's terms and privacy policy;
- **your AI agent platform** (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) — governed by its provider's terms;
- any additional MCP server **you** configure per the Skill's setup documentation.

We are not a party to, and have no visibility into, any of these data flows.
